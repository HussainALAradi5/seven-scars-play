Privacy policy: Seven Scars

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Seven Scars is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No server: the game never sends your data anywhere
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

What stays on your device

Your settings and game progress (difficulty, unlocks, scores) are saved only on your device: in the browser's local storage on the web, and in the app's own storage on mobile. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes them.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains stylized fantasy violence. See its age rating in your app store.

Changes

If the game ever adds online features, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the change will be shown in the game.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
