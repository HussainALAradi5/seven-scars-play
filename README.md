# Seven Scars: playable demo

**Play in your browser:** https://hussainalaradi5.github.io/seven-scars-play/

A fast 2D action brawler: 8 playable chapters with chases, hazard runs and locked arenas, 5 enemy types, 23 bosses, combos, grabs and throws, ground-slam shockwaves, limited ammo and timed buffs, story cutscenes with Finish-or-Spare choices, per-chapter music, four difficulties and three art styles (Comic, Pixel, Noir). Desktop (keyboard or gamepad) and phones in landscape (touch).
