# Seven Scars: playable demo

**Play in your browser:** https://hussainalaradi5.github.io/seven-scars-play/

A fast 2D action brawler demo: human fighters, combos, a heavy finisher, pistols, dodge, waves, four difficulties, a hidden Blood Moon wave on Hard and Scarred, three art styles (Comic, Pixel, Noir), sound with a mute button, a chapter select (chapter 1 playable), and full-width play on wide phones. Works on desktop (keyboard or gamepad) and on phones in landscape (touch controls).
